# Gameplay rules

These are the rules as built. They come from [further-gameplay-features.md](further-gameplay-features.md)
and the follow-up decisions. Code references are in brackets. All numbers are in `config.py` (`Tuning`).

## Teams, players and roles

- Two humans, one per team. **Blue** (`Team.LEFT`, the host) defends the left end zone. **Red** (`Team.RIGHT`, the joining player) defends the right one. Teams never swap ends; only who has the ball changes.
- A **coin flip** decides who starts on offense.
- There are always three players on the field [`rules/teams.py`]:

| Role | Team | Controlled by | Can |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QB** | Offense | Offense human, during the play call only | Throw (automatically). Can't move, be tagged, or have the ball taken. |
| **WR** | Offense | Offense human, during execution | Run, slide, wall-jump, catch / pick up (Z). After an interception, tag the Defender (Z). Can't throw. |
| **Defender** | Defense | Defense human | Run, slide, wall-jump, catch / pick up (Z). Tag the WR when the WR has the ball (Z). Can't throw. |

- Z does whatever applies: it tags if an opponent holding the ball is within `tag_radius` (40px), and otherwise tries to pick up the ball. Tagging a player without the ball does nothing.
- Players pass through each other.

## The field

- **Size:** every field is 100 yards, with a 10-yard end zone at each end [`rules/field.py`]. Bigger levels use more pixels per yard, set by the level's `yard_px` (the default is 24px, where the player is about 2 yards tall). The 10- and 20-yard spawn rules scale with each level automatically.
- **Levels:** all are generated by `tools/gen_levels.py`. Each one's left half is designed and mirrored, so neither team has an easier route. A test checks that every surface can be reached from the floor.

| Level | Size (px) | Yard | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| `field_01` Field 01 | 2,944 × 1,080 | 24px | Knee-high blocks, a double-jump wall, a slab to slide under, tiers of one-way ledges, a midfield wall with a bridge |
| `field_02` Skyline | 3,424 × 1,500 | 28px | Buildings with fire-escape ledges, a wall-jump shaft between towers, a high rooftop route, a midfield skyscraper to climb over or slide under |
| `field_03` Caverns | 3,664 × 1,200 | 30px | Low ceiling, hills and a pit, a tunnel with a crawlspace, stalactites, a wall-jump chimney, a central column with a mid-height window |
| `field_04` Archipelago | 3,904 × 1,800 | 32px | The tallest. Solid islands you reach from the side, stepping stones, a hanging wall to climb, a central chimney up to a summit and sky platform |

  Choose one with `--level field_03` (the host's choice is sent to the joining player).
- **Field markings** on every field, in matches and in sandbox mode:
  - end zones tinted in the defending team's color, with the team name painted on the floor
  - goal lines, the 50, lines every 10 yards and ticks every 5 yards
  - yard numbers painted above the floor and pinned near the top of the screen
- **Camera and minimap:** the window (1,280 × 720) follows what you control, and a minimap across the top shows the whole field.
- **Where players can spawn:** a player can only spawn standing on top of a platform where they fit, never floating or on a wall.

## A Down

A **possession** is up to **4 downs**. Every down is played the same way, a play call and then an execution. The only difference between downs is **how the QB's spawn is decided** [`rules/down.py`]:

| Down | QB spawn (`SpawnRule`) |
|---|---|
| 1st down of a possession: match start, after any touchdown, after a turnover on downs | `ChooseInRegion`: the offense places the QB anywhere **from its goal line to its 10-yard line**. The defense watches the placement live. |
| 1st down after an interception | `FixedSpot`: where the interceptor was tagged (the last platform they touched). |
| Downs 2-4 | `FixedSpot`: where the last play died (rules below). |

There is no "first down by gaining 10 yards". The offense gets exactly 4 downs to score.

### Where the Defender may spawn

The Defender must spawn **at least 10 yards past the furthest point the QB could be**, toward the defense's end zone. The defense's **own end zone is always allowed** [`defender_x_range`].

- On a 1st down where the QB is placed, the QB can be up to the 10-yard line, so the Defender must be at the **offense's 20-yard line or further back**. This boundary is fixed, so it doesn't move while the QB is dragged.
- On a fixed-spot down, the boundary is 10 yards past the QB's spot. If that is inside the end zone, the whole end zone is allowed.

## Phase 1: Play call (15s) [`rules/play_call.py`]

| | Offense | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| **Place** (only when the down lets the offense pick) | Drag the QB with the mouse, or move it with the arrows. It snaps to the nearest platform top in the yellow zone. **Z / "Confirm QB spot"** confirms. | Drag the Defender, or move it with the arrows, inside your zone. **Z / "Lock in Defender"** locks in. |
| **Aim** | Hold the mouse and point: the direction is the angle (full circle) and the distance is the power, up to 200px for max. Or use left/right for angle and up/down for power. **X / "Lock in throw"** locks in. Clicking the QB (or Z) goes back to placing. | |

- **Who sees what:** the QB placement is visible to both sides as it moves. The throw angle and power are **hidden from the defense**, and the Defender's spot is **hidden from the offense** until execution. Each player is only ever sent their own view, so the hidden data never reaches the other machine [`net/view.py`].
- **Starting values:** the last values that team used. The very first time, the QB is mid-region, the aim is 45° toward the opponent at middle power, and the Defender is 5 yards past its boundary.
- **Both locked in:** execution starts immediately.
- **Timeout:** if 15s pass without both sides locked in, the **play call restarts** for the same down. Positions, aim and any lock already made are kept. A "(play call restarted)" note is shown.

## Phase 2: Execution [`session.py`]

1. The QB, WR and Defender spawn. The WR starts next to the QB, toward the defense's end zone.
2. **10 seconds of free running.** The QB holds the ball.
3. The QB **throws automatically** with the locked-in angle and power.
4. **Either player can catch it** (Z within reach; catching is forgiving, see the README).
5. If the ball **stops moving** (it has come to rest and is slower than 8px/s), there are **3 seconds** to pick it up. Otherwise the play is dead. Picking it up within that window makes it live again.
6. **WR has the ball:** the WR heads for the defense's end zone, and the Defender tries to tag the WR.
7. **Defender has the ball (interception):** the Defender heads for the offense's end zone, and the WR tries to tag the Defender.

**Reaching the end zone** means the carrier is **standing on a platform** inside the opponent's end zone. Jumping through the end zone doesn't count.

### How a play ends, and what follows [`next_down`]

| Ending | Score | Next |
|---|---|---|
| WR reaches the end zone: **touchdown** | Offense +1 | Other team's 1st down, QB placed by them |
| Defender tags the WR | | Next down from the WR's last platform. **After the 4th down: turnover on downs.** |
| Ball not picked up in time: **dead ball** | | Next down from the **same QB spot** (like an incomplete pass). After the 4th down: turnover on downs. |
| WR tags the Defender after an interception | | Defense becomes the offense: **1st down at the tag spot** |
| Defender reaches the end zone: **pick six** | Defense +1 | The team that was scored on gets the ball back: 1st down, QB placed |

- **"Last platform touched":** the last position where the player was standing on a platform. Wall-clinging doesn't count. So a player tagged in mid-air is spotted where they last stood.
- **Spot inside your own end zone:** a fixed spot there is pushed out to the goal line.
- **Pause:** each result is shown for 2.5s before the next play call.

## Winning [`rules/match.py`]

A touchdown is worth 1 point, and the first team to **5** wins. The host presses R to start a new match.

## Modes

| Command | What it is |
|---|---|
| `python -m ballbrawlla` | **Practice:** you are always on offense. The Defender is placed automatically and never moves. |
| `python -m ballbrawlla --host` / `--join <ip>` | A 2-player match over the local network. The match starts when both are connected and restarts if someone leaves. |
| `python -m ballbrawlla --sandbox` | The original free-play arena (no rules), for tuning movement. |

## Interpretations and open questions

These are choices made where the spec allowed more than one reading. Each one is a single setting or a small change if you want it different.

- **Defender spawn rule:** "20 yard line or further back" is implemented as *10 yards past the QB's furthest possible spot*. That gives the offense's 20-yard line on a placed 1st down, and the same 10-yard gap on later downs.
- **Play call timeout:** keeps any lock already made, so only the side that hadn't locked in still needs to.
- **After an interception:** the new offense's 1st down starts at the tag spot instead of letting them place the QB.
- **Balance to playtest:**
  - At 320px/s a player runs about 13 yards per second, so the 10-second pre-throw is long enough to cross most of the field on flat ground. The obstacles slow this down, but `pre_throw_seconds`, `run_speed` and `yard_px` may need tuning.
  - The field layout is a first pass for medium difficulty and hasn't been playtested.
